## 1. What this project is
An original animated comedy channel (workplace, marriage, friendship, money, facts/psychology, hypotheticals) with a recurring stickman cast, built on top of the open-source `stickman-video-director` Skill and its local Node/FFmpeg renderer. The owner approves everything; ChatGPT is the creative and architectural decision partner; Claude Code is the technical and creative production agent.
