### What the locked identity means for content
Every episode, whatever the pillar, uses **simple expressive 2D characters in richly illustrated cinematic 2D environments** in the canonical treatment: workplace stories in offices, relationship stories in apartments and restaurants, money stories in shops and homes, facts and psychology explainers in illustrated settings, hypotheticals in any illustrated world. **No photographic or photorealistic settings, and no flat-cartoon exceptions.** Settings serve the story and **the world at large**; the visual treatment never changes. New recurring characters follow the same-universe rule (`VISUAL_IDENTITY.md` §5) and need the owner's approval.

## 0. Platform strategy (APPROVED 2026-11-11; details PROPOSED)

The brand publishes on **YouTube, TikTok and Facebook**. Content is **original and international**; African or Nigerian storytelling must not define the identity. Full architecture and research: [MULTI_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md](MULTI_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE.md).

| Platform | Primary role | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | Short-form discovery | 9:16, up to 3 min (official) | Titles up to 100 characters (official) |
| **TikTok** | Short-form discovery | 9:16 | Specs and AI-label rules not yet confirmed on official pages |
| **Facebook Reels** | Short-form reach | 9:16 | Meta's originality guidance (March 2026) favours original, creator-made content |
| **YouTube long-form** | Episodes, depth | 16:9, 2–8 min | Home of complete episodes |
| **Facebook video** | Secondary distribution | 16:9 where suitable | Scope to be decided (M14) |

**Per episode:** one complete original episode; two or three vertical short-form edits (recomposed, not cropped); a platform-specific hook, caption style, title, description and thumbnail or cover for each platform; safe framing checked with overlay guides. **Do not upload identical files, captions or lengths everywhere**, and vary the edits so cross-posts are not duplicates.

**Pillar emphasis by format:** short, single-gag ideas (workplace, relationships, everyday frustrations, money) suit vertical first; explainers (facts and psychology) and hypotheticals suit 16:9 episodes with vertical cut-downs. The six pillars and topics below remain valid **only where they are universal**; any topic or example written for a West African setting is superseded (see the banner above).

## 5. Cadence and pipeline [PROPOSED, D17: to be re-planned per platform]

> The cadence below was drafted for YouTube only. With three platforms and vertical-first output, cadence, batching and cut-down counts must be re-planned (decision D17 is open). Treat the numbers as placeholders.


- **After the pilot is accepted:** one standard episode and three Shorts per week would be the **ceiling**, not the plan, until the reuse library exists. Start at **one episode every two weeks plus two Shorts per episode** and increase as pose libraries mature.
- **Shorts from episodes:** every episode yields 2–3 Shorts cut as native vertical recomposes (not crops).
- **Batching:** write four scripts at once; record all voices for a block; render in batches (the renderer caches unchanged shots).
- **Series structure:** each marriage episode stands alone; the "Mummy" saga is the only multi-part series at launch.

## 6. Audience and growth notes [PROPOSED]

- Titles state the situation, not the joke ("When Your Wife Says We Need to Talk").
- Thumbnails show **one big reaction face** plus 2–4 words, in the brand colours.
- First 5 seconds of every video must work muted.
- Always-on captions for accessibility and international viewers.
- A pinned comment asks a relatable question ("What is the scariest 'We need to talk' you have received?").
- Playlists by pillar and a "Start here" playlist introducing the cast.

## 7. Risk notes

- **Repetition:** the biggest risk to a formula channel. Mitigated by the Joke Ledger and rotation rules (`COMEDY_WRITING_GUIDE.md` §12).
- **YouTube rules:** repeated formats can trigger "repetitious content" scrutiny. Keep original scripts, distinct plots and human editorial control; disclose synthetic or altered content where YouTube's current policy requires it (check the policy before launch).
- **Fact accuracy** in the facts pillar: sources and a verification pass.
- **Cultural sensitivity:** see `COMEDY_WRITING_GUIDE.md` §9.
