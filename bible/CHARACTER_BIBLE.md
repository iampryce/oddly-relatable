## 1. Femi Balogun — main male

**Age range:** 32–36 (34) · **Job:** Operations Coordinator at Brightline Logistics · **Signature colour:** Teal #17A398

**Personality:** kind, loyal and anxious. A planner who believes enough preparation can prevent any disaster, then prepares for the wrong disaster. Funny without trying.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Devoted to Amara, reliable at work, generous with friends, genuinely good at fixing logistics problems |
| **Weaknesses** | Catastrophises, avoids direct conflict, over-apologises, tight with money in odd places while splurging on gadgets |
| **Habits and quirks** | Rehearses arguments in the shower (and wins them). Keeps a drawer of every charger he has ever owned. Names his spreadsheets. Checks the electricity meter like a stock price. Hides gadget purchases in "work" boxes. |
| **Sense of humour** | Self-deprecating, observational, dad jokes he finds hilarious |
| **Speech patterns** | Starts sentences with "Okay, so…". Over-explains when nervous. Speeds up under stress. Says "it's fine" when it is not. Occasional Nigerian English ("I'm coming" meaning "wait here"). |
| **Typical reactions** | Freezes, then over-acts. Sweat drop escalates. Pre-emptive apology. Imagines the worst-case scenario in full detail. |
| **Relationships** | Adores Amara and slightly fears her raised eyebrow. Tunde is his best friend since university. Tolerates Dez. Terrified of disappointing Madam F. Wants Mummy Eze's approval and never quite gets it. Pepper ignores him. |

**Visual appearance:** domino head (tall rounded rectangle), skin S3, low-cut flat hair, **thick round glasses** (lenses flash white when he is "processing"), slim build, 3.6 H tall.
**Clothing:** Teal polo with a small collar (work and casual), lanyard at work; grey shoes. Home: faded teal T-shirt.
**Default brows:** inner ends slightly raised (worried at rest).
**Distinctive expressions and poses:** "processing" glasses glint; hands-together pleading; finger-counting his own mistakes; frozen vertical posture; the "everything is fine" thumbs up with a twitching eye.

**Potential recurring jokes**
- The Worst-Case Theatre: his imagination frame shows absurd catastrophes in grey.
- The charger drawer, which contains everything except the charger he needs.
- He wins every argument in the shower and loses every real one in under 10 seconds.
- "It's fine" tally counter.
- The secret gadget purchases (the drone).

## 2. Amara Balogun — main female

**Age range:** 30–34 (32) · **Job:** Hospital Pharmacist · **Signature colour:** Tangerine #FF7A3C

**Personality:** direct, organised and quick-witted. Says exactly what she means and expects others to do the same. Underneath, deeply sentimental. She is the one who keeps every cinema ticket. Competitive about everything.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Decisive, honest, protective, brilliant in a crisis, remembers everything |
| **Weaknesses** | Remembers *everything* (and brings receipts), hates admitting she is wrong, tests people with loaded questions, avoids telling her mother "no" |
| **Habits and quirks** | Labels food in the fridge with names and dates. Plays board games to win. Writes sticky notes for Femi and hides them in his bag. Runs the household budget in a ring binder. Puts on reading glasses when she is about to make a serious point (she does not need them). |
| **Sense of humour** | Deadpan and dry; devastating one-liners; laughs hardest at Femi when he panics, then comforts him |
| **Speech patterns** | Short sentences. Questions that are traps ("Did you or did you not…?"). Calls Femi by his full name "Oluwafemi" when it is serious. Code-switches into sharper Nigerian English with Zee. |
| **Typical reactions** | **One raised eyebrow** (signature). Slow blink. Arms crossed with a tapping foot. A tiny smile when she has won. |
| **Relationships** | Loves Femi and finds his anxiety both endearing and exhausting. Zee is her closest friend and worst influence. Tolerates Tunde ("your consultant"). Adores her mother and cannot say no to her. Pepper is her ally. |

**Visual appearance:** egg head (wider at top), skin S2, **afro puff** on top, small gold hoops, 3.3 H tall.
**Clothing:** Tangerine wrap top with darker trousers (casual); white pharmacist coat over Tangerine at work; Tangerine ankara-print dress for occasions.
**Default brows:** level, slightly low (calm authority).
**Distinctive expressions and poses:** the single raised brow; the slow blink; "the folder" pose (arms on the table, ring binder open); victory micro-smile; hand-on-hip lean.

**Potential recurring jokes**
- The ring binder of household evidence ("Exhibit C: the towel").
- The fake reading glasses come out for serious talks.
- Labelled leftovers, which Femi always eats anyway.
- She cannot say no to her mother, who therefore keeps visiting.
- She is secretly the more romantic one, and gets caught.

**Personality:** loud, loyal and supremely confident. Single and proud of it, yet the self-appointed relationship consultant to everyone. His advice is terrible; his friendship is not.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Loyal, generous, fearless, can fix any phone, a natural salesman |
| **Weaknesses** | Overconfident, gives advice outside his expertise, allergic to commitment, always "on his way" |
| **Habits and quirks** | Always eating something (suya, plantain chips, a meat pie). Calls everyone "my guy". Quotes made-up proverbs as ancient wisdom. Has a different "girl" every episode whom we never see. |
| **Sense of humour** | Big, physical, roasts friends lovingly, laughs at his own jokes first |
| **Speech patterns** | Loud. Sprinkles Pidgin ("Wetin happen?", "No wahala"), always clear from context. Proverbs: "As my grandfather said, a man who explains is a man who is losing." |
| **Typical reactions** | Spits out his drink, laughs until he falls off his stool, puts an arm around the victim, gives a thumbs up to obviously bad ideas |
| **Relationships** | Femi's best friend since university. Amara thinks he is a bad influence but likes him. He and Zee are rivals who may or may not secretly like each other (slow-burn subplot). Sells Dez overpriced phone cases. |

**Visual appearance:** circle head, skin S1, **Lime bucket hat**, stocky, wide stance, 3.2 H tall, chunky watch.
**Clothing:** white T-shirt with a Lime stripe; Lime trainers.
**Default brows:** high and relaxed (confident).
**Distinctive expressions and poses:** the advice-giving pose (one hand on chest, one finger raised); the laughing fall; arm-around-the-shoulder; eating mid-sentence.

**Potential recurring jokes**
- "As my grandfather said…" invented proverbs.

## 11. Balance rules for relationship humour [PROPOSED; supports the approved balance requirement]

1. **Rotate who is "wrong".** Across any three marriage episodes, each partner is the cause of the problem at least once.
2. **Both are competent.** Femi is anxious, not stupid. Amara is direct, not nagging. Jokes target the behaviour, never the gender.
3. **Shared ending.** Most marriage episodes end with both partners on the same side, often against an outside problem (Mummy's visit, the landlord, the cat).
4. **No contempt.** They can tease, argue and plot; they never belittle each other.
5. **Track it.** A simple per-episode note records who caused the conflict and who "won". Review every five episodes.
